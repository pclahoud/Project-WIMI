Subject vs Exam Weight Analysis

iCompares your share of mistakes in each subject with the exam's weight for it. A subject's mistakes include everything beneath it. A subject is over-represented when your share is above 1.3 times the middle of its weight band, outside the band, and it has at least the minimum number of mistakes set in Settings (3 to start with). Over-represented subjects worth 10% or more are Priority; lighter ones rank below them, even ones you miss often. It counts mistakes, not your accuracy, so it reads true when you practise mixed blocks: blocks filtered to one subject inflate that subject, and a subject you have not practised shows no mistakes.
