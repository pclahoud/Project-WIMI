Your subject weights come from two places: some are copied from the official exam blueprint, and others you typed in or WIMI worked out from the ones around them. The efficiency score can't tell the difference — it treats every weight as if it were published. Turn this on and the score is shown as a range instead of a single number, widened by how much of your exam's weight was never taken from a blueprint. The score itself doesn't change; only how much precision it claims.

Two copies have gone their own way

Two of your computers both changed this profile without seeing each other's work. Nothing is lost - both copies are safe in the folder, and WIMI saves a backup of this computer's copy before doing anything at all. WIMI will not merge them; you choose which to keep.

Your choice applies to this computer straight away. The other computer is not told until you press Send - so you can check everything here first.
