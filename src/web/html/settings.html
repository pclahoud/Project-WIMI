Your subject weights come from two places: some are copied from the official exam blueprint, and others you typed in or WIMI worked out from the ones around them. The efficiency score can't tell the difference — it treats every weight as if it were published. Turn this on and the score is shown as a range instead of a single number, widened by how much of your exam's weight was never taken from a blueprint. The score itself doesn't change; only how much precision it claims.

Microphone

Which microphone WIMI records through. This choice stays on this computer: it names a piece of hardware plugged in here, so it is not carried to your other machines.

Speech model

The model does the recognising, and it is downloaded once rather than shipped with WIMI. A bigger one recognises more technical terms and takes longer to run. Measured on this project's own corpus: small.en-q5_1 recognised 84% of the terms at about half of real time, small.en 85% for two and a half times the download, and base.en 75% for a third of the size. Like the microphone, the choice stays on this computer — the file it names lives here.

Starting…

Changing model keeps the old one, so changing back costs nothing. Remove one when you are sure you are finished with it.

Recognition

Before each recording WIMI gives the recogniser a short piece of prose built from the subjects on the entry you are writing, so it knows to expect those words. It is the difference between hearing a drug or an eponym correctly and getting something that sounds like it. Turn it off only if it is putting words into your transcripts that you did not say.

Two copies have gone their own way

Two of your computers both changed this profile without seeing each other's work. Nothing is lost - both copies are safe in the folder, and WIMI saves a backup of this computer's copy before doing anything at all. WIMI will not merge them; you choose which to keep.

Your choice applies to this computer straight away. The other computer is not told until you press Send - so you can check everything here first.
